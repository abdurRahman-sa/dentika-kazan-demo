Scope: standalone concept mockup `dentika/design/concept-v3-standard.html`. Visitor mode: Persuade (real present-day audience: the clinic owner judging the demo).

Audience & job: a Kazan patient (or a parent booking a child) picks a time without phoning; the owner judges whether this beats the sites they are compared against.
Proof on hand: real 2ГИС 4.9/81 and Яндекс 5.0/45, real address and phone, real clinic photos, real service list. Placeholders stay marked demo.
Constraints: ст. 24 ФЗ "О рекламе" (visible contraindication notice, no superlatives/comparisons/guarantees), no human or animal faces, 152-ФЗ consent checkbox + privacy link, booking is simulated.

Four palettes ship in one file behind a review toolbar; block content is identical across them.

## Direction contract

THESIS: the category standard for a dental landing, executed at federal-medtech craft, refusing the local-clinic habit of a decorated brochure with a "leave a request" form.
OWN-WORLD: strict 12-column grid on a near-white ground, one cool brand hue for structure and actions, one warm accent rationed to what a state polyclinic cannot offer, authored line icons at a single stroke weight, real clinic photography, tabular figures.
STORY: the visitor sees a clinic that publishes its hours, ratings and open times, understands they choose doctor and slot themselves, and books without a phone call.
FIRST VIEWPORT: header with phone and booking action; left column headline, one paragraph, primary and phone actions, a three-item verified-facts row; right column a large real treatment-room photo carrying the live free-slots pill in the warm accent.
FORM: the standing exit (category canon), taken by the user over the dealt roll; seed key a51d4e17.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

Unresolved: whether one palette becomes the client-facing default; DESIGN.md still describes the incumbent Досье world and is untouched by this concept.
